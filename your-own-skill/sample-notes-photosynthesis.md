## 1. What photosynthesis is

- Photosynthesis is how green plants make food: they use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.
- Overall equation: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.
- It takes place mainly in the leaves, inside organelles called **chloroplasts**, which contain the green pigment **chlorophyll**.

## 2. Two stages

- **Light-dependent reactions** (thylakoid membranes): light splits water, releasing oxygen, and produces ATP and NADPH.
- **Light-independent reactions / Calvin cycle** (stroma): ATP and NADPH are used to build glucose from carbon dioxide.

## 3. Factors that affect the rate

- **Light intensity:** more light speeds it up, up to a point where another factor becomes limiting.
- **Carbon dioxide concentration:** more CO₂ speeds it up, up to a limit.
- **Temperature:** too cold slows the enzymes down; too hot denatures them.
- **Limiting factor:** the factor in shortest supply controls the overall rate.
