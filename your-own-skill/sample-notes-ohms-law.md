## 1. Ohm's law

- **V = IR**, where V is voltage (volts), I is current (amperes), and R is resistance (ohms).
- Ohm's law says the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it, provided the temperature stays constant.

## 2. Resistance

- Resistance depends on the material, the length, and the cross-sectional area: R = ρL / A, where ρ is resistivity.
- A longer wire has more resistance; a thicker wire has less resistance.
- A resistor converts electrical energy into heat. Power dissipated: P = VI = I²R = V²/R.

## 3. Series and parallel

- **In series:** the same current flows through each resistor; total resistance adds up: R = R1 + R2.
- **In parallel:** the voltage across each resistor is the same; total resistance is less than the smallest resistor: 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2.
